Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 30, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your personal information when you use the Protein Note application (hereinafter referred to as "this app").

1. Information We Collect

This app may collect the following information:

  • Nickname (for user identification)
  • Nutrition intake tracking data (protein, calories, fat, carbohydrates)
  • Goal setting information (nutrition targets, weight, calculation method)
  • Step count data (via Apple HealthKit / Google Health Connect)
  • Walking distance data
  • Calories burned calculated from steps
  • App usage data (for analytics features to be implemented in the future)
  • Device information (OS, device type, etc.)

1.1 Nutrition Record Data

We record and store the following nutrition data:

  • Food name and intake amount (grams)
  • Protein amount (grams)
  • Calories (kcal)
  • Fat (grams)
  • Carbohydrates (grams)
  • Record date and time
  • Body weight

1.2 Health Data Collection

This app collects the following health data with your consent:

For iOS - Data from Apple HealthKit:

  • Step count data
  • Walking and running distance

For Android - Data from Google Health Connect:

  • Step count data
  • Distance data

This data is used only for the following purposes:

  • Displaying daily steps and calories burned
  • Displaying weekly and monthly step statistics
  • Analyzing the balance between nutrition intake and exercise consumption

Important:

  • Health data is accessed only when you grant permission
  • Permission can be revoked at any time from your device settings
  • Health data is processed on your device, and only daily summaries (steps, distance, calories burned) are stored on the server

2. Purpose of Use

The collected information will be used for the following purposes:

  • To provide the basic functions of the app (nutrition recording, statistics display, goal management)
  • Analysis and visualization of nutritional balance
  • Automatic calculation of weight-based nutrition goals
  • Retrieving step data and calculating calories burned
  • Statistics display and analysis of exercise volume
  • To improve the user experience
  • To improve and optimize the app
  • To provide notification features to be implemented in the future
  • To resolve technical issues

3. Storage and Management of Information

  • Your nutrition record data is encrypted and stored on our servers.
  • Data is managed under appropriate security measures.
  • Some data (such as settings, goals, weight) is stored locally on your device.
  • When using guest mode, all data is stored only on your device.
  • Row-level security (RLS) is applied to nutrition record data, preventing access from other users.
  • Step data is retrieved from HealthKit/Health Connect on your device each time.
  • Daily step summaries are encrypted and stored on the server.
  • Detailed step data (such as hourly breakdowns) is not sent to the server.

4. Provision to Third Parties

We will not provide your personal information to third parties, except in the following cases:

  • With your consent
  • When required by law
  • When necessary to protect the life, health, property, or other significant interests of you or the public

5. Analytics

In the future, we may collect anonymized analytics data to improve the app. This data will be used only for statistical analysis and will be processed in a way that does not identify individuals.

6. Notifications

The notification feature, to be implemented in the future, may send you reminders and update information based on your settings. You can disable notifications at any time in the settings.

7. Data Deletion

You can request to delete your account at any time. Upon receiving a deletion request, we will delete your data within a reasonable period.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be revised as necessary. If there are significant changes, we will notify you within the app or by other appropriate means.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the Support Page.
